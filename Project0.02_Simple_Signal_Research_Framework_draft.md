# Draft for Simple signal research framework

## This is mainly a continuation to the Project0.01, I plan to implement more of what I am learning during my undergraduate, as well as other concepts to do a more deep dive  

### Main goal of project
The main goal is to reasearch different signals that have been very popular in the industry, such as Momentum, Mean reversion, Volume ratio, RSI.
- Build the signal
- Use Information coefficient (Spearman's rank correlation) to measure it's predictive power
- Test statistical significance using different tests 
- Look at the values based on different market situations (not really sure how to do this, need more research)
- Provide a conclusion for each signal bases how they perform under different tests and scenarios 
- Finally give a few thought of limitations and possible improvement of the methods used
